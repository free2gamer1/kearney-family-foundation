Our story

Serving the Kearney community since 2003.

The Kearney Family Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit corporation founded in November 2003. Every penny donated — 100% of all donations — goes directly to the children and families we help. Volunteers and area businesses donate all of our operating costs.

Our mission

The Kearney Family Foundation is dedicated as a community of individual volunteers to assisting the children and families of the Kearney School District. Our administration is handled entirely by a volunteer Board of Directors, allowing us to operate with no administrative fees so that all contributions focus on what matters most: helping others.

Every year during the holiday season, KFF and its numerous volunteers come together to benefit the citizens of Kearney, Missouri — shopping, wrapping, and delivering gifts to families in need. Our primary delivery day typically falls a few weeks before Christmas each year.

Volunteers like you and area businesses donate all of our operating costs, so every dollar you give goes straight to a family.
